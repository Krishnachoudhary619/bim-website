APPLICATION DOMAINS

Scan-to-BIM Expertise Across Every Sector

From complex historic masonry to hyper-dense industrial process piping, our engineers execute workflows customized to sector-specific constraints.

01

Residential Projects

Historic restorations, luxury single-family estates, and multi-family high-density developments. Capturing intricate architectural cornices, irregular walls, and structural shifts with laser precision.

Focus: Heritage Preservation, As-Built Verification
02

Commercial Spaces

Commercial office towers, retail fit-outs, corporate headquarters, and mixed-use complexes. Delivering precise core, shell, and tenant space layout modeling for seamless fit-out coordination.

Focus: Space Optimization, Tenant Improvements
03

Infrastructure & Civil

Transit hubs, subway stations, pedestrian & vehicular bridges, tunnels, and civil earthworks. Coordinated with geospatial GIS coordinates for comprehensive regional planning.

Focus: Georeferenced Coordinates, Civil 3D
04

Industrial & Process Plants

Petrochemical refineries, process piping networks, manufacturing plants, and heavy equipment rooms. Exact diameter, flange, and centerline tracing for maintenance and expansion engineering.

Focus: Piping Centerlines, Spool Drawings

Utilities, Telecom & Energy

High-voltage electrical substations, renewable energy solar/wind farms, and telecommunications antenna mount towers with precise wind-load surface mapping.

Focus: Equipment Clearances, Structural Rigidity
